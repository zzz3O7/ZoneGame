How to play
Goal

Claim more zone points than your opponent by the time neither of you can move.

The board

Each match starts on a procedurally generated cave. Only floor cells can be played on. Some wall cells hold bonus markers.

Placing pieces

On your turn, place one piece on empty floor: a domino, tromino, tetromino. Dominoes are limited each game; trominoes and tetrominoes are unlimited.

Creating a zone

Placing a piece on cells that aren't part of any zone yet creates a new zone around it. That zone is now contested between you and your opponent.

Contesting a zone

Once a zone exists, you and your opponent take turns adding pieces inside it — each piece must fit entirely within that zone's cells, and only on your turn to act there.

Winning a zone

If it's your turn to act in a zone and you have no legal move left inside it, the zone is completed — and your opponent scores its full value (1 point per cell, +5 per bonus marker).

Passing

If you have no legal move anywhere on the board, you must pass — but it costs you 30% of your current score.

End of game

The match ends once neither player can move. Whoever has the higher score wins — equal scores are a draw.
